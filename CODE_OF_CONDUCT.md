# Code of Conduct

Kardano SDK welcomes contributors, reviewers, users, and pilot partners from varied backgrounds. Participate with respect, focus discussion on the work, and assume good intent while asking for clarification when something is unclear.

## Expected behaviour

- Be constructive in issues, reviews, discussions, and community spaces.
- Critique designs and contributions without attacking people.
- Respect confidentiality around vulnerability reports, pilot conversations, and non-public
business information.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and deliberately disruptive behaviour.



## Reporting

For conduct concerns, email [sarmidev@outlook.es](mailto:sarmidev@outlook.es). For technical
vulnerabilities, follow [docs/SECURITY.md](docs/SECURITY.md) instead.

Reports will be handled privately where possible and with attention to the information provided.
